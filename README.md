# NAVI
### Navigation & Advancement Via Intelligence

**A practical guide to managing your job search through ChatGPT. No coding required.**

Job hunting can turn into a pile of browser tabs, different resumes, forgotten questions, and confirmation emails. NAVI gives you a repeatable way to keep it together with ChatGPT, a spreadsheet, and private folders.

You tell ChatGPT about your experience and the jobs you want. NAVI provides the instructions and checklists to help it compare roles, draft truthful application materials, organize records, and identify your next step. You check the facts and decide what to submit.

## Start here

Open **[START HERE — Set up NAVI in ChatGPT](START_HERE.md)**. You can read the guide on GitHub and copy its instructions. You do not need to install NAVI, write code, or understand GitHub.

Start with one job description and your resume. Connecting email and Google Drive is optional; the guide explains both connected and manual paths. Some ChatGPT capabilities depend on your account, plan, and available tools. The basic process can still be done by supplying selected documents and making file or spreadsheet updates yourself.

## What NAVI helps you do

| Job-search task | How NAVI helps |
| --- | --- |
| Find a sensible next role | Compare actual requirements with your experience, pay needs, location, and preferences. |
| Prepare your application | Draft a tailored resume, cover letter, and answers using supported facts. |
| Stay organized | Keep a job description, document versions, answers, and receipts in one private application folder. |
| Avoid duplicate applications | Check the company and job's reference number before adding a new record. |
| Know your status | Keep a tracker with evidence, dates, follow-ups, and a next action. |
| Prepare for an interview | Return to the posting and the exact answers and materials you used. |
| Learn from your search | Review which kinds of roles and approaches lead to interviews, without overreading a small sample. |

These are guided tasks carried out with you and ChatGPT. Downloading this guide does not connect your accounts or start a background service.

## What you need

- A ChatGPT account.
- A resume and truthful notes about your skills, experience, and achievements.
- A private spreadsheet, such as Google Sheets.
- A private folder for your files, such as Google Drive.

You can paste a job description or upload selected files. If supported, you can authorize connections so ChatGPT can find email evidence and update your tracker or archive. Reading and editing are different capabilities: ask ChatGPT to check what it can actually do.

## Your first request

> I am using NAVI, Navigation & Advancement Via Intelligence, to organize my job search. Help me set up using the attached instructions. Ask me for the minimum information needed, explain each step simply, and work on one role at a time. Use only verified experience. Do not submit applications, send messages, delete files, overwrite originals, or publish materials without my approval.

## The simple routine

1. **Check the role.** Verify the official job posting and whether it fits.
2. **Prepare.** Tailor your materials and save the questions and final answers.
3. **Review.** Check the actual application form and attached files.
4. **Submit when ready.** You control submission.
5. **Confirm and track.** Save evidence, update the existing tracker row, and set a next action.

A prepared resume is not proof of submission. An automatic confirmation is not an interview. Silence is not rejection.

## Stay in control

ChatGPT can be wrong. Check qualifications, dates, personal answers, required yes/no questions, and attachment names before submitting. It cannot promise interviews, offers, or employer responsiveness. A project does not automatically grant access to your computer, inbox, or files. Scheduled checks need supported access and a separately confirmed schedule.

NAVI is an independent community workflow for using ChatGPT. It is not an OpenAI product or an employer application platform. No custom server, API key, Python installation, or paid hosting is required for the guided workflow. Optional public decks may involve separate hosting choices; you can apply without a deck.
