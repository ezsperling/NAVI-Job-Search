# Requests you can copy into ChatGPT

A prompt is just a request. Replace the words in brackets with your details. Supply the current standard, profile, and source files; ChatGPT must not pretend it can see a private account or folder it cannot access.

## Set up my search

> Help me set up NAVI, Navigation & Advancement Via Intelligence, for my job search. Use START_HERE and the application standard. Ask for my preferences and verified experience, explain unfamiliar terms, and work one step at a time. Help me create a private tracker and folders. Check which actions you can perform and give me manual alternatives for missing access. Do not submit, send messages, publish, delete, change sharing, or overwrite originals without my approval.

## Find the next role

> Using my current profile, help me choose one strong role. Verify an open official posting, job reference number, location eligibility, salary when available, and key requirements. Check existing applications and excluded employers. Explain proven fit, transferable fit, and gaps. Save the complete JD and a checklist, then prepare truthful materials. Mark it Preparing, with no applied date until supported.

## Tailor a resume and answers

> Tailor my approved resume for [role] using verified achievements only. Preserve the approved layout and links. Do not invent metrics or credentials. Save a new version. For every application question, save the exact prompt, limit, and my final answer in the role folder. Ask me about personal facts you cannot know. Complete a required cover letter even if a deck exists.

## Check before I submit

> Compare this final application review page with my approved materials. Check all parsed job titles, employers, dates, education, current-employment selections, required yes/no answers, internet/logistical requirements, contact details, links, and attachment filenames/versions. Flag unknowns or inconsistencies. Save the review evidence if available. Do not click Submit.

## I finished submitting

> I submitted [company/role/reference] on [date/time/time zone if known]. Here is my confirmation, or check my authorized email for the matching receipt. Update the existing tracker row without duplicates and save submitted-material evidence, all final answers, and the receipt reference in the role folder. Distinguish filename evidence from exact-file verification. Assess all 18 checklist items and report what remains missing. Set a next action and a follow-up review respecting employer timelines.

## Recover older applications

> Reconcile [authorized folder/email/date range] against my tracker. Separate files merely prepared from verified or candidate-reported submissions. Match company plus job reference number, retaining distinct roles. Read relevant full threads and sent replies for current outcomes. Preserve originals. Do not infer application dates from cloud upload dates or rejection from silence. Leave unknown dates blank unless I explicitly authorize a labeled file-creation-date proxy. Report gaps, what you checked, and the next action.

## Review my inbox now

> Review my authorized job-search email since the last successful check with overlap, including weekend mail or any missed period. Read full relevant threads and sent replies. Separate submissions and decisions from job alerts, drafts, scams, and unrelated roles. Update verified applications and evidence records under my internal-recordkeeping authorization. Preserve tracker layout and avoid duplicates. Do not send, archive mail, change labels, or publish/delete anything. Report status changes, deadlines, replies owed, and gaps; record the successful check window. This is a one-time review, not a request to create a schedule.

## Set up weekday checks, if supported

> I want weekday NAVI inbox reviews at [time and time zone] using [account] and my tracker. First check whether scheduled tasks can use the required email and file tools in my account. If supported, create the schedule and confirm the actual task details; if not, explain the manual alternative. Include weekend mail on Monday and missed periods after failures. Update internal records only within my authorization, never send messages or submit applications, and report failures. Do not claim a schedule exists until the scheduling tool confirms it.

## Correct a submitted answer

> I found [error] in my submitted application for [role/reference]. Preserve the original review evidence and record the issue. Check official portal editing options if authorized. Do not withdraw or resubmit automatically. Prepare a correction message only if needed, verify its recipient, and wait for my approval before sending. Add a preventive check for future applications.

## Prepare for an interview

> Use the saved posting, exact submitted resume/answers where verified, and invitation for [role] to prepare concise examples and questions. Separate supported accomplishments from practice suggestions. Record interview date/time zone and deadlines, save notes privately, and update my next action. Do not send replies or accept invitations without authorization.

## Review my results

> Review interview conversion by role category, source, resume version, and deck usage. Use confirmed applications and explain missing data or small samples. Do not claim that a deck or wording caused an interview from correlation alone. Recommend a focused adjustment and check any deck retirement reviews against the latest substantive hiring update.
